[h1]Version 2.0.2[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Sky in the water[/b]: [i]Live sky[/i] or [i]Cubemap[/i]. With Cubemap the water reflects a cubemap mod's sky (Lumina, Dynamic Cubemaps, Cubemap Replacer), as bright as the live sky. Water tab.
[*][b]Lightning in the water[/b]: with Volumetric Weather 1.6.0 or newer, its bolts and flashes reflect in the water.
[*][b]Room light colors[/b]: each building gets one main white with a few rooms in another, and a few buildings are lit in color. Interiors tab.
[*]The building window (Alt+G) has a new [b]Interiors[/b] tab: a building's own light color, second color, lit rooms, brightness and rooms by day. Asset creators can set them too.
[*]If a game update breaks something the mod needs, it switches itself off with one message. Your settings and cities are kept.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]The panel shows every tab and setting to everyone: [i]Show advanced options in the in-game panel[/i] is gone.
[*][i]Reflect the sky like real water[/i] is now [i]Reflect the sky[/i]. [i]Sky reflection[/i] goes up to 100% and is 50% for new installs; a higher value you saved is kept until you move the slider.
[*]Dirt is much stronger: 100% now shows clearly patchy grime.
[*][i]Keep the painted detail[/i] also shows a building's painted curtains and blinds through its glass by day.
[*]New defaults for new installs and [i]Reset all settings to defaults[/i]: Reflective blue glass, rooms shown by day at 30%, room lights at 150%. Your own settings and profiles stay as they are.
[/list]
[b]Removed[/b]
[list]
[*][i]Panes follow the weather[/i] and [i]Light shafts[/i] under the water: both are always on now.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]Dark blotches and colored specks in the water's reflections under bright low clouds.
[*]Reflections in the water no longer trail behind while you move or turn the camera.
[*]Thin bright lines round trees and terrain, and blue lines along window edges, with a dynamic resolution mod above 100%.
[*]A thin pale line along the water's edge at banks, quays and piers.
[*]The water no longer turns washed out and gray when it rains.
[*]Neighbors' facades no longer jump in brightness when you zoom toward towers.
[*]A city whose painted shores were damaged could never be painted again.
[/list]
